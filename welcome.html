HappySubs translates the captions a video already has. On Vimeo and Bilibili there's nothing to switch on.

✓ Saved
  1. 1

    Pin it to the toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon by the address bar, then the pin next to HappySubs.

  2. 2

    Open a video, click CC

    Bottom right of the player. Refresh any video page that was already open.

  3. 3

    Both languages appear

    Both appear together, one line at a time. Change the language any time from the toolbar icon.

Worth knowing

  • No captions on a video at all? Live transcription writes them as it plays.
  • Turn on Translation only in the popup to read just the translation, with CC off.
  • Hover over the subtitle and a handle appears — drag it up or down, full screen included.
  • Download what you're watching as an .srt, in both languages or either one.
