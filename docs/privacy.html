Privacy Policy for HappySubs

Last updated: October 5, 2026

HappySubs is an unofficial browser extension that adds a second subtitle overlay while you watch YouTube, Vimeo and Bilibili videos, and can optionally transcribe videos that have no subtitles at all. This extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube, Google, Vimeo or Bilibili.

Data We Store

The extension stores your settings in Chrome extension storage, such as selected subtitle language, subtitle style, translation provider, which of that provider’s models to use, how you want a paid API to be used, and optional API keys that you enter for third-party translation services. This data is stored in your browser through Chrome storage and is not sent to any server operated by the extension developer.

It also keeps a small counter on your device: how many videos you have watched for at least a minute with both subtitle languages showing, which of its few requests to rate it on the Chrome Web Store it has already made, and whether you have rated it. It uses this only to decide when to ask, and when to stop asking, and it never leaves your browser.

Data Sent to Third Parties

When you use translation features, subtitle text may be sent directly from your browser to the translation provider you choose, such as Google Translate (reached at clients5.google.com or translate.googleapis.com, with no account or key), OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, or DeepSeek. If you enter an API key for one of these services, that key is used only to call the selected provider from your browser.

Live Transcription and Audio

Live transcription is an optional feature that is off unless you install a separate local program and switch it on in the extension. While it is running, the extension reads the audio of the video you are watching and sends it over a loopback connection (ws://127.0.0.1:8765) to that program, which runs on your own computer and returns recognised text.

The audio does not leave your computer. It is not uploaded anywhere, not recorded to disk by the extension, and not sent to the extension developer or to any third party. Nothing is captured while the feature is switched off, and the extension never accesses your microphone — only the audio of the video element on the page you are watching.

The recognised text is then translated the same way subtitle text is: sent to whichever translation provider you have chosen.

Feedback You Choose to Send

When the extension is first installed, it opens a welcome page that is part of the extension itself; nothing is sent when it opens. When the extension is removed, Chrome opens a short survey page on this website. The survey is optional: nothing is sent unless you choose a reason and press Send. If you do, what is sent is the reason you chose, anything you typed into the boxes, the version of the extension you removed, and the language the page was shown in. It is stored in a Google Form owned by the extension developer and used only to decide what to improve. It does not include your name, email address, browsing history or any identifier for you or your browser.

Data We Do Not Collect

The extension collects no usage statistics or analytics of any kind, and the developer does not operate a backend server for it. Apart from the optional uninstall survey above, the extension sends nothing to the developer. The developer does not collect your browsing history, does not sell user data, and does not share user data with advertisers or data brokers.

This Website

The HappySubs home page at huanshuowang.com/happysubs/ uses Google Analytics to count its visits in aggregate. It records how many people visit, which site or search sent them, whether the short film on the page reached its middle or its end, whether its sound was turned on, whether the page was switched to English or Chinese, and which “Add to Chrome” button was clicked. To do this, Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and receives your browser and device type and your IP address, which it uses to estimate your country and city and does not store. The developer sees only aggregated reports, does not use them to identify anyone, and does not use them for advertising. How Google handles this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Only the home page uses Google Analytics. The uninstall survey page and this privacy policy do not, and none of this applies to the extension itself. You can block it with a content blocker or with Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

Permissions

Every host the extension asks for corresponds to something it actually does:

  • www.youtube.com, vimeo.com, player.vimeo.com, www.bilibili.com — the pages the extension runs on, to read the video's subtitles and draw the overlay.
  • captions.vimeo.com, api.bilibili.com — where those two sites publish their subtitle files, which the extension fetches to translate.
  • translate.googleapis.com, api.anthropic.com, api.openai.com, generativelanguage.googleapis.com, api.deepseek.com — the translation providers, contacted only for the one you have selected.

Live transcription needs no host permission: it talks to a program on your own machine over a loopback address.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete extension data by removing the extension from Chrome or clearing the extension's stored data in your browser. You can also remove saved API keys from the extension popup.
